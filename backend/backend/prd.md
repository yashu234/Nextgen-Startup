# Product Requirements Document (PRD)

## Project Overview
**Startup Forge** is an AI-powered platform designed to help entrepreneurs and founders convert a raw startup idea into a comprehensive, actionable startup launch kit.

## Targeted Users
- **Aspiring Entrepreneurs**: Individuals with a business idea who need structure and guidance.
- **Hackathon Participants / Innovators**: Teams needing rapid business plan generation.
- **Consultants**: Professionals looking for a starting point for client business proposals.

## Core Features
1. **Idea Ingestion**: Form-based input capturing Startup Idea, Industry, Target Audience, Budget, and Business Type.
2. **AI-Powered Generation**: 
   - Business Plan
   - Branding Guidelines (colors, typography, mission)
   - Website Content Preview
   - Marketing Strategy
   - Financial Projections & ROI
   - Pitch Deck Structure
   - Compliance & Legal Recommendations
3. **User Authentication**: Secure signup and login to save progress.
4. **History & Analytics**: Dashboard to view, download, and manage previously generated startup kits.
